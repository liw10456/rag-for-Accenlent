# Nimbus Cloud Security and Compliance

## Access control

Nimbus uses role-based access control with three built-in roles: Viewer, Developer and Admin. Only Admins can manage billing, invite members or delete projects. Single sign-on through SAML is available on the Enterprise plan.

## Data protection

All customer data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.3 and at rest with AES-256. Nightly backups are retained for 14 days on Starter and Team plans and for 35 days on Enterprise.

## Compliance

Nimbus holds a SOC 2 Type II report, which customers can request through the Trust Center under NDA. Nimbus does not currently sign Business Associate Agreements, so it should not be used to store protected health information.

## Reporting vulnerabilities

Security researchers can report vulnerabilities to the security team through the responsible disclosure form. Nimbus aims to acknowledge reports within two business days.
