# Nimbus Cloud Billing Guide

Nimbus Cloud is a fictional hosting platform used as sample data for this project.

## Plans

Nimbus offers three plans. The Starter plan costs 12 dollars per month and includes 2 vCPUs, 4 GB of RAM and 50 GB of SSD storage. The Team plan costs 49 dollars per month and includes 8 vCPUs, 16 GB of RAM and 250 GB of storage. The Enterprise plan is priced by contract and adds a dedicated account manager and a 99.99 percent uptime SLA.

## Invoices

Invoices are generated on the first business day of each month and are emailed to the billing contact. Customers can download past invoices as PDF from the Billing page for up to 24 months.

## Refunds

Nimbus refunds unused time on annual plans on a prorated basis if the request is made within 30 days of renewal. Monthly plans are not refundable, but customers can cancel at any time and keep access until the end of the billing period.

## Failed payments

If a card payment fails, Nimbus retries the charge three times over seven days. After the final failed retry the account is moved to read-only mode. Data is kept for 30 days in read-only mode before deletion.
